# Code of conduct

waydlp is a community project. We want everyone who uses or contributes to it to feel welcome and safe. This code of conduct is based on the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

* Be kind, patient and respectful, especially to newcomers.
* Assume good intent and ask before assuming.
* Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
* Focus on what is best for the community and the people using waydlp.
* Take responsibility for mistakes and learn from them.

## Unacceptable behaviour

* Harassment, insults, slurs or personal attacks.
* Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention.
* Publishing others' private information without permission.
* Trolling, deliberate derailing or sustained disruption.
* Asking for help bypassing DRM or accessing content you aren't allowed to access.
* Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies to all project spaces: repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions and any official channel. It also applies when someone represents the project publicly.

## Enforcement

Report problems privately to the maintainers. Use the "Report content" feature on GitHub, or a private security advisory in the relevant repository if no other private channel is available. Reports are handled confidentially.

Maintainers may take any action they consider appropriate. Depending on severity, this ranges from a private warning, to a temporary ban, to a permanent ban from project spaces.
